Why this is suggested

2025 AHA Adult Cardiac Arrest Algorithm · Synthetic simulation only; clinician determines and documents all actions.

High-quality CPR recommendations

  • Push hard: at least 2 inches (5 cm).
  • Push fast: 100–120 compressions per minute.
  • Allow complete chest recoil; minimize interruptions; avoid excessive ventilation.
  • Change compressors about every 2 minutes, or sooner if fatigued.
  • Without an advanced airway: 30:2 compression-to-ventilation ratio.
  • With an advanced airway: continuous compressions and 1 breath every 6 seconds.
  • Use continuous waveform capnography when available; if ETCO₂ is low or decreasing, reassess CPR quality.
Open the AHA source PDF

Shock recommendations

  • For VF/pulseless VT, recommend one shock, then immediately resume CPR for 2 minutes before the next rhythm check; do not recommend stacked shocks.
  • Biphasic: use the manufacturer's recommended energy (commonly 120–200 J); if unknown, use the maximum available setting. Subsequent shocks may use equivalent or higher energy according to device guidance.
  • Monophasic: 360 J.

Informational only. The app does not select device settings or deliver a shock; the clinician determines and documents the action.
